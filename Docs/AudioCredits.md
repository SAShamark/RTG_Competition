# Audio sources

All third-party audio is CC0 (public domain); attribution is not required, listed here for traceability.

- Kenney — Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio (kenney.nl, CC0): doors, hatches, metal mechanisms, footsteps, items, UI.
- OpenGameArt "fireplace sound loop" and "fire crackling" (CC0): fire loop, sizzle, fire out.
- OpenGameArt "150 Combat Sound Effects" (CC0, compilation): explosion, wood/glass breaks, gas burst.
- OpenGameArt "Park ambiences" — birds (CC0): street ambience bed.
- Juhani Junkala — JRPG Music Pack #4 [Calm], "Peaceful Days" (CC0): background music.

Clips were trimmed, layered, pitched, filtered and loudness-matched; gas and extinguisher hiss beds are procedural noise layered with the recordings above.
